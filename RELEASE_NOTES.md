## What's Changed

**Preserve exact AGL tariff rates**
AGL Battery Rewards setup and options now accept rates to two decimal places and preserve them when tariff settings are reopened and saved. Rates such as 6.63 c/kWh no longer fall back to a whole-cent value, including tariffs with an explicit off-peak period and generated uncovered hours.

Update available via HACS
