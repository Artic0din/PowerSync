## What's Changed

**Keep each ZeroHero Super Export day independent**
The 48-hour optimizer now gives every local calendar day its own export bonus allowance. Today uses only its remaining daily allowance, while future days receive their full configured allowance, preventing tomorrow's window from absorbing today's remaining opportunity.

**Preserve daily limits and export safety**
PowerSync still enforces the configured cap separately for each day and keeps the existing battery reserve, site limit, no-import window, and execution price protections. The allowance remains a daily maximum rather than a guaranteed export amount.
