## What's Changed

**Planned charge costs now count only paid grid energy**
The 24-Hour Optimizer Plan now separates forecast solar from grid-supplied battery charging when it calculates Charge-window energy and estimated cost. Solar-assisted charge windows no longer price the battery's entire charge rate as grid energy, while schedules from older or fallback data sources retain their existing display.
