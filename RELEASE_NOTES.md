## What's Changed

**Spread Import now preserves solar energy needed for charging deadlines**
Smart Optimization no longer replaces forecast solar-charging self-consumption slots when spreading deliberate battery charging across an equal-price import window. Charge By Time and Profit Maximisation plans now retain the solar energy already counted by the optimiser instead of gradually increasing grid charging as the target approaches.

Same-price boundaries, site import limits, battery charge limits, free-import handling, and priority export planning remain enforced.
