## What's Changed

**Show tariff rates consistently to two decimal places**
Tariff rates now display with exactly two decimal places across the Action Plan, price charts and tooltips, import and export gauges, LP forecast summaries, tariff schedule state, and custom tariff period summaries. This keeps rates such as 24.00 and 6.63 visually consistent without changing stored values, currency conversion, optimizer inputs, or API data.
