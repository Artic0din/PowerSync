## What's Changed

**Choose the Happy Hour window that matches your Flow Power plan**
Flow Power setup and options now let you select either the 5:30pm-7:30pm or 5:30pm-9:30pm Happy Hour window shown on your account's plan or energy price fact sheet. Existing installations keep the 7:30pm end time unless changed. The selection now stays aligned across tariff schedules, current export pricing, cost tracking, and Smart Optimization export and charge decisions, while Flow Power PEA import pricing remains unchanged.

Update available via HACS
