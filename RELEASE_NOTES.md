## What's Changed

**Historical energy flow now includes battery state of charge**
The PowerSync dashboard's Energy - 24 Hours chart now overlays battery SOC on a dedicated 0–100% right axis alongside Solar, Grid, Battery, and Home power. Power traces keep their existing kW scale, while the legend and tooltips show the correct unit for each series on desktop and narrow screens.
