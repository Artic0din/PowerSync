## What's Changed

**Current Week costs now agree with Day on the first day**
When a live Week, Month, or Year contains only today, PowerSync now uses the same live optimizer cost breakdown shown in the Day summary. This prevents identical energy totals from showing different costs on Mondays or the first day of a month or year, while preserving safe tariff-based estimates for multi-day history.
