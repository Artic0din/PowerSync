## What's Changed

**AGL setup supply charge**
The AGL Battery Rewards setup now asks for daily and monthly supply charges alongside the plan's peak and off-peak feed-in rates. This keeps the fixed charge visible during the AGL pricing flow instead of requiring new users to find it separately under Demand Charge tracking.

**Existing cost tracking stays connected**
The new AGL fields save to the same daily and monthly supply-charge settings already used by PowerSync's demand-charge cost tracking, so configured charges continue to feed the existing cost sensors and can still be adjusted later from options.
