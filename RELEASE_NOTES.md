## What's Changed

**Disabled GloBird spike export now stays disabled**
PowerSync no longer starts AEMO spike checks or force discharges when GloBird is selected and the spike export setting is turned off. Smart Optimization now honors the same optional spike-export toggle as the other control paths, including after Home Assistant restarts or PowerSync reloads.
